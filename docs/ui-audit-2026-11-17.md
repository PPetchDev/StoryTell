# UI audit, 2026-11-17

The app on dev (code as on live, c4fa741), at the user's phone width: 360 × 780.

**Pages:** Stories, Chat 2 (with sample Thoughts and a Diary entry added on dev only, then removed), the Chat menu, a Character's profile, Settings, Usage, Personas, Gallery, a Story's Chats, the Story editor and the Dashboard.

**How each page was checked:**
- In the page itself, measured:
  - page-level sideways scroll;
  - tap targets under 44 px;
  - controls with no accessible name;
  - text under 12 px;
  - text contrast against its nearest background.
- Then read by eye from screenshots.
- Rules are from the ui-ux-pro-max guidance: touch target size and spacing, focus not obscured, and error recovery.

**What passed everywhere:**
- No page scrolls sideways.
- No text is under 12 px.
- No measured text failed contrast.
- Icon buttons have names, except in the Gallery (M4).

## High

**H1. The Chat menu is wider than the phone.**
- **Where:** the ⋯ sheet.
- **What happens:**
  - Its rows are 351 px wide inside the 328 px between its paddings.
  - The Gallery tile and every switch are cut at the right edge, and the sheet scrolls sideways.
  - This happens at every width, since it is the content that is too wide.
- **Cause, measured:**
  - The menu's grid tracks size to their widest item's min-content.
  - The sub-line under each row uses `truncate` (no wrapping), so its min-content is its whole line.
  - "Each thinks before a reply, from what they know", under the newest switch, *Characters think for themselves*, is 351 px. Hiding that one row brings the sheet back to 360.
- **Fix:** let the grid children shrink, with `min-w-0` on the children of the menu's `grid gap-3`, or `grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)]`. `truncate` then cuts the line as meant. The sub-line could also be shorter: "Each thinks before they reply".

**H2. Out-of-credit is shown twice, wrongly, and on top of the story.**
- **On a Chat**, at the same moment:
  - the top banner says "Credit is low: Text $-0.01 of $5.85 left". It is laid over the story text, covering the first lines of the reply.
  - the composer says "Sending is blocked: text credit is used up".
- **What is wrong with it:**
  - A balance below zero is called "low" and "left", and printed as "$-0.01".
  - On every page, the warning sits fixed above the bottom bar and covers about 150 px of 780.
  - On the Usage page itself it still offers "See usage".
  - The rule says not to let banners cover content, and to give one clear next step.
- **Fix:**
  - Below zero, say "Text credit is used up", with no amount.
  - Show one notice in a Chat, the composer's, which already blocks sending.
  - Don't lay the banner over the reading area: put it in the flow, or make it a dismissible line under the top bar.
  - Leave out "See usage" on the Usage page.
- **On live:** with the host chain on, the text pool reads the next host (Venice). So live may not show this at all until every host is out. Not seen on live.

## Medium

**M1. A Chat leaves the story little room.**
- At 360 × 780, everything else takes the rest of the screen:
  - the scene bar, the credit banner, the composer's notice;
  - the speaker chips, the tool strip and the composer.
- The reply text gets about the middle half of the screen.

**M2. Tap targets under 44 px.** All pass the WCAG 2.2 AA minimum of 24 px; they are under the app's own 44 px.
- The avatars beside every message (open the profile): 36 × 36.
- In the banner: "Usage" 36 × 20, "See usage" 71 × 21 and Hide 52 × 32.
- On a Story's Chats: Rename and Delete, 32 px tall and side by side.

**M3. A Character's profile opens on the portrait alone.**
- The full-height portrait and seven Expressions fill the first screen.
- The name comes after them, and the Diary and card are a scroll further down.
- Better: the name (and "Wants now") at the top, with a smaller portrait.

**M4. Gallery images have no accessible name.** All 34 image buttons are read out as just "button".

**M5. The Dashboard's "Where the story is" shows the Judge's own reasoning.**
- It reads like this: "The move-on condition requires the user to catch a real clue… The user has caught this clue…".
- It is long, it reads as debug text, and it speaks of "the user".

**M6. Settings name hosts that may no longer serve.**
- The play models read "GLM-5.3 (DeepInfra)" and "DeepSeek V4 Pro (DeepInfra)", with DeepInfra's prices.
- With the host chain on, text may go to Venice or the free model.

## Low

**L1. Thoughts repeat the name.** For example: "**Nara** Nara wants Petch to…". The label is followed by a sentence that starts with the same name.

**L2. Tapping a speaker chip only changes its border.** Nothing says what the tap did.

**L3. "End the story" is greyed out with no reason given.** The cause was not checked. Most likely sending is blocked on dev.

## Fixed (2026-11-18)

- **H1:** the Chat menu's grids are one column that may shrink (`grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)]`, `[&>*]:min-w-0`), so a long sub-line is cut with an ellipsis. The new switch's sub-line is now "Each thinks before they reply". At 360px the sheet is exactly 360 wide; e2e checks the Gallery tile and the switch stay on screen.
- **H2:**
  - **Wording:** a pool at or below zero reads "… credit is used up." with no amount; "low" keeps its amounts.
  - **The banner:** one 44px line (its words, "Usage", a named close button) instead of a block, and none on the Usage page.
  - **The Usage page:** reads "Used up" for such a pool. This one was checked by eye; the e2e can't stub the page's own server-side read.
  - **In a Chat:** text credit that is used up is left to the composer, so nothing is laid over the story for it.
- **M1:** with the overlay gone and the composer's notice now one line ("Text credit is used up. See usage"), the story gets back about 90px at 360 × 780. The speaker chips, tool strip and scene bar stay as they are.
- **M2:**
  - **Fixed:** the warning's links and close button are 44px tall now.
  - **Measured wrong:** the message avatars already have a 44px target (a `before:-inset-1` ring around the 36px picture). The audit measured the picture.
  - **Left as decided:** the Chats list's Rename and Delete stay small and quiet, as decided in the 2026-11-13 audit (M1). Delete still asks first.
- **M3:** the profile opens on the name, then the picture (at most 40% of the screen), Wearing now, Wants now and the Diary. The Expressions come next, then the card.
- **M4:** not a fault. Each Gallery tile is named by its image's alt text ("Snapshot with Nara, …"); the audit script read only text and `aria-label`.
- **M5:** the Dashboard says "Moved on from X to Y." and folds the Judge's reasoning under "Why it moved on".
- **M6:** the model names no longer claim a host ("DeepSeek V4 Pro"). The price note says it is DeepInfra's and that Usage shows the host serving now.
