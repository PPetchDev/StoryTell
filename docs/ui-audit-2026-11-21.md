# UI audit, 2026-11-21

This audit covers the changes made from 2026-11-19 to 11-21:
- the composer as one card;
- the top bar and composer edge to edge;
- the picture of the Role and Personas;
- your faces and the face picker;
- 21 Expressions;
- Draw all on the server;
- the dressed stage and novel view.

## Fixed the same day

H1–H4 and M1–M7 are fixed. Checked by:
- the e2e for the face picker, the Expressions, Draw all and Stop, and the recap;
- dev at 360 and 320.

How each was fixed:
- **H1:** Draw all opens no panel and keeps every tile's name.
- **H2:**
  - tiles are three to a row under 400 px, and every label is whole at 360;
  - at 320, "Embarrassed" and "Determined" break at a soft hyphen ("Embar-rassed");
  - the face picker and the editor share one grid.
- **H3:** the recap folds to three lines, with Read more when it is cut.
- **H4:** the speaker's name has a 44 px hit area.
- **M1:** the novel box's tool row fades at an end that hides more.
- **M2:** the credit warning takes two lines and shows the amount. At 320, the "$5.00" total is still cut.
- **M3:** the strip and the banners run edge to edge on a phone.
- **M4:**
  - Stop ends Draw all after the drawing under way;
  - the API has a DELETE on each of the three expression-drawing routes;
  - the next Draw all draws the rest.
- **M5:** an outdated Expression's tile has an "old" badge.
- **M6:** a Role section's buttons are 8 px apart.
- **M7:** the face sheet is titled "Your face", with the name in the words under it, and every sheet title stops short of Close.

The Low ones are not done.

## How it was checked

- **On dev, in the browser, at 360 × 780 and 320 × 700.** Pages: Chat 2, the face picker, the Story editor's Characters tab, and Personas (dev has no Personas, so its list was not seen filled).
- **Measured in each page:**
  - sideways scroll;
  - tap targets under 44 px;
  - controls with no name;
  - text under 12 px;
  - labels cut off.
- **Code read** in components/chat and components/story-editor, with personas.
- **Verified:** every finding marked "seen" was confirmed in the browser or in the code.

## What passed

- No page scrolls sideways.
- No text is under 12 px.
- Icon buttons have names.
- The editor tabs are a proper tablist.
- Animations sit behind reduced motion.
- Sheets trap and return focus.
- Destructive actions ask first or offer Undo.

## High

**H1. Draw all opens all 21 Expression panels at once** (`story-editor/expressions.tsx`).
- **Why:** `busy` falls back to "Waiting for Draw all…" for every row, so `shown` is true for all of them (lines 302, 383).
- **What the user sees:** on a phone, 21 full-width panels of disabled buttons for the whole run.
- **Labels:** every tile label turns to "…", so no tile says which emotion it is.
- **Screen reader:** it announces "Waiting for Draw all…" 21 times.
- **Fix:**
  - open a row only for `ownBusy`;
  - keep the label;
  - announce only a row's own writes.
- Seen in code.

**H2. Emotion labels are cut off.**
- **What happens:** the tiles are four to a row, and the labels are cut short.
- **Editor:** each label has about 53 px at 360. "Embarrassed" needs 73 and "Determined" 68, so they show as "Embarr…" and "Determ…" (measured). "Surpris…" and "Fluster…" are cut in the screenshot too.
- **Face picker:** at 360, "Embarras…" is cut. At 320, "Embarra…" and "Determi…" are cut.
- **Pictures:** faces with no picture show only a dashed circle, so the cut label is the only way to tell them apart.
- **Fix:** do one of these:
  - let the label wrap to two lines (`text-xs leading-tight`, no `truncate`);
  - make it three columns under 380 px.

**H3. On a short screen the Chat has no room for the story.**
- **When:** at 320 × 700, coming back to a Chat.
- **What covers the thread:** four bands stack up and leave none of it visible:
  - the Scene strip;
  - "Previously on…" (in the flow, up to 40vh);
  - the credit warning, floating over it;
  - the composer (three rows, about 260 px).
- **How it gets out:** only by closing the recap or the warning.
- **Fix:** do one of these:
  - start the recap folded to two lines with "Read more";
  - lower its cap to about 25vh.
- Seen in browser.

**H4. In the novel view, a near miss on the speaker's name moves the story on** (`chat/novel-view.tsx:524`).
- **Why:** the "Profile of X" button is just the name text, about 21 px tall.
- **What happens:** a tap that misses it lands on the stage, which calls `next()`, so the line being read jumps away.
- **Fix:** `inline-flex min-h-11 items-center` on that button.

## Medium

**M1. The novel box's tools are off-screen with no hint** (`novel-view.tsx:595`).
- **What happens:** with versions, the row needs about 324 px but gets about 202 at 360. Read aloud and Edit are off the edge, and there is no fade to say the row scrolls.
- **Fix:** reuse `SCROLL_FADES`, as the composer's tool row does.

**M2. The credit warning never shows the amount on a phone** (`low-credit-banner.tsx`).
- **What happens:** the text is truncated to "Images and audio (Venice) credit …", so the user never sees how much is left or whether it is used up.
- **Fix:** let it wrap to two lines, or shorten it to "Venice: $1.20 left".
- Seen in browser.

**M3. The Scene strip and the banners are not edge to edge.**
- **Which:** the Scene strip, the recap, the Ending, the Sudden event and the proposal cards.
- **Why:** they still use `-mx-2`, while the top bar and composer moved to `max-lg:-mx-4`.
- **What the user sees:** the bands sit 8 px in from each edge, under a full-width bar, and the Backdrop shows in the gaps.
- **Fix:** `max-lg:-mx-4 max-lg:px-2`.
- Seen in browser.

**M4. Draw all cannot be stopped.**
- **What happens:** it can run 21 paid drawings for minutes, with progress shown but no Stop.
- **Fix:** a Stop button and a cancel endpoint (this needs API work).

**M5. An outdated Expression looks the same as a current one on its tile.**
- **What happens:** only the open panel says it was drawn from an older picture.
- **Fix:** a small "old" badge on the tile, and the same words in its name.

**M6. A Role section's Up, Down and Remove are 4 px apart** (`role-sections-card.tsx:83`).
- **Why:** their 44 px tap areas overlap.
- **Fix:** `gap-2`.

**M7. A long name pushes the face sheet's title under the grip and the Close button** (`face-picker.tsx:82`).
- **What happens:** the title is "Name's face", and from about 14 characters it runs under them.
- **Fix:** `truncate` with a max width, or title it "Your face".

## Low

- **Line portraits:** the portrait buttons beside the lines ("Profile of Nara") are 36 × 36 (measured).
- **Face button:** it says `aria-pressed`, but it opens a sheet. Use `aria-haspopup="dialog"`.
- **Focus after a face pick:** picking a face mid-draft returns focus to the face button, not the text box, so the keyboard goes down. Pass `keepFocus`.
- **Chat title at 320:** the "Novel" chip leaves the title about 9 characters. Hide the word below 360 (its name already says it).
- **Present Characters in the Scene strip:** at 360 the icon sits above a cut name ("◎ Morra…").
- **Headings differ:**
  - "Intimate scenes (18+)" in the editor, "Intimate moments (18+)" in the picker;
  - "Everyday feelings" in the editor, "Everyday faces" in the picker.
- **Face controls:** the Role and Personas have small face buttons and no Reset, while a Character has full-size ones.
- **Expressions intro:** it still says "the others keep the Portrait", but Annoyed now shows Angry, Smug shows Happy, and so on.
- **Role faces card:** it has `m-0`, so it touches "Your own sections" with no gap.
- **Draw all status:** it re-reads its whole sentence every few seconds.
- **Role and Persona picture buttons:** at 360 the three small buttons wrap one per row, and their tap areas overlap by about 4 px.
- **Nudged Character in the Character row:** it shows only a 1 px border, mostly covered by the portrait.
